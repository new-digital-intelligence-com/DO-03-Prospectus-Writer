# Meridian Endpoint Care — service specification (internal, approved 12 Aug 2026)

Meridian Endpoint Care is a managed endpoint service for mid-sized professional firms.

Tiers
- Meridian Essential: monitoring and patching, business hours. Per endpoint per month.
- Meridian Complete: adds remote remediation and asset lifecycle. Per endpoint per month.
- Meridian Complete Plus: adds on-site attendance. Per endpoint per month.

Commercials
- Contract term: 36 months
- Notice period: 90 days
- Pricing basis: per endpoint per month, billed monthly
- Minimum engagement: 250 endpoints
- Service level: 99.5% platform availability; 4 business hours to first response

Measured outcomes from the 2026 internal review
- Patch compliance moved from 71% to 96% across the managed base.
- Mean time to remediate fell from 6.2 days to 1.4 days.
